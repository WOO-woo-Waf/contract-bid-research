# Repository instructions

This repository is a durable research base for AI-assisted contract review and bid/tender document generation.

- Keep third-party clones under `references/`; do not vendor or commit their working trees.
- Record every upstream URL, resolved commit, license, access result, and reproduction command in `references/UPSTREAMS.md`.
- Separate article/vendor claims from capabilities verified in source code or a running system.
- Date formal research documents as `YYYY-MM-DD` and keep them under `docs/design-architecture/contract-bid-research/`.
- Do not store contracts, tender files, credentials, personal data, or customer data in Git.
- Legal conclusions require qualified human review; AI output is decision support, not a legal opinion.
